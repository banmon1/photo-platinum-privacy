Photo Platinum privacy policy

Publisher: Angmon.io. Updated November 24, 2026.

Editing on your device

The editor opens photos and projects you select on your device. Preferences, a generated player ID, presets and recovery data can be stored on the device. You can clear these through your device or browser storage settings. Files you export remain wherever you save them.

Drawing together

Online Play connects to a drawing server for matchmaking, room codes and reconnection. The server receives connection information including your IP address and processes session identifiers, room activity and drawing commands to show your strokes to the other player. Only draw things you want that player to see. The built-in room service keeps game state in memory and expires inactive rooms. If you choose a custom server, its operator handles that connection and its logs.

Purchases and sharing

Your app store handles checkout under its own privacy policy. Photo Platinum has no advertising SDK. When you share exported work or invitations, the app or recipient you choose receives what you share.

Questions

Contact Angmon.io through the developer contact on the store listing or open a privacy inquiry. GitHub issues are public. Please omit personal details, room codes and artwork.
